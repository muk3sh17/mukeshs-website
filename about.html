Story

I started in 2016 writing Swift as an iOS developer. Building apps taught me how products are made. Watching people use them taught me why some succeed.

At Ameex Technologies I moved from code into product and UX. I set up the company's first in-house product practice and delivered enterprise healthcare products under HIPAA and WCAG requirements.

As a senior designer at ZF Wabco and Brillio, I worked with global product owners on fleet management and on products for Verizon and Terminix. At WNS I led Optima AI as Product Manager, from day zero to production.

Today I'm looking for Product Manager and AI Product Manager roles where data and AI change how people work. I also build with AI every day, using Claude Code, Codex and Lovable to prototype and ship faster.
