How I work

From discovery to outcomes

01

Discover

Go where the work happens. On Optima AI that meant three weeks onsite with 20+ executives and 8+ operations teams before writing a single story.

02

Define

One north-star metric, clear KPIs and a prioritised backlog (RICE, Kano). Discovery became 150+ user stories and a phased release plan.

03

Deliver

Agile delivery with cross-functional teams of 25+. When timelines tighten, I re-scope the MVP — not the outcome.

04

Measure

Live dashboards over manual reports. 450+ legacy reports became 60 self-serve Power BI dashboards.

05

Learn

Stop on evidence, not hope. I paused PetalPost after testing exposed friction in its core loop.

Where AI fits: rule-based automation first, then AI where it can replace manual decisions — like the 18+ AI use cases I mapped for Optima AI.
