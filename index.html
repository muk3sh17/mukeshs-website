Listen

Every product starts as noise.

Every team hands me noise: scattered requests, conflicting opinions, spreadsheets and workarounds nobody wrote down. I don’t filter it too early. I listen inside it — sitting with the people who do the work until the real problem is louder than the opinions about it.

Shape

I find the shape in it.

One north-star metric, clear KPIs and a prioritised roadmap. Discovery becomes user stories and a phased release plan — rules first, then AI wherever it can replace a manual decision.

Then I ship it.
